Check a flood alert

Scan the QR code on an UrbanPulse flood poster to open this page. Your phone recomputes the alert's fingerprint from the alert text in the link, then points you to the public Shardeum blockchain record where the same fingerprint was sealed. No UrbanPulse server is contacted.

Check the blockchain record

  1. This fingerprint was computed on your phone from the alert text.
  2. Open the sealing transaction on the Shardeum explorer.
  3. Check that "Interacted with contract" is the UrbanPulse contract
  4. Tap "View details". Under "Decoded input data", the call is anchor and arg0 must equal the fingerprint above.
